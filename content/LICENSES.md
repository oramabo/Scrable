# Content sources and licences

| Dataset | Path | Source | Licence / status |
|---------|------|--------|------------------|
| Hebrew clue/answer pairs | `content/he/clues/clues.jsonl.gz` | Mordo clue dictionary (pitaronfree.blogspot.com), fetched through the public Blogger feed by `tools/content/scrape_mordo.py` on 2026-10-28 | Imported as drafts (`status: imported`). Clue phrasings are short dictionary-style definitions published openly; the compilation is the blog's. Treat as reference material until an editor approves or rewrites each clue (`status: approved`). Legal review recommended before shipping imported clue text verbatim. |
| Hebrew lexicon | `content/he/lexicon/words.jsonl.gz` | Answer words extracted from the same dictionary; difficulty derived from how many clues each word answers | Word lists of this kind are factual data; the derived difficulty scores are ours. |
| Layout templates | `content/he/templates/*.json` | Generated by `tools/generate templates` (simulated annealing) | Ours. |
| Puzzles | `content/he/puzzles/**` | Generated by `tools/generate pack|dailies` from the lexicon and clues | Ours; carry `meta.generator` (version, seed, template, dictionary snapshot) for reproducibility. |

Not used (and why): Hspell word lists are AGPL and never shipped; published newspaper puzzles are copyrighted and
were not scraped.
